# Pick Your Cue

Pick Your Cue is a hands-on activity. You practice picking a cue for a task that keeps getting stuck, and you build a prompt to paste into an AI chatbot when you stall.

**Try it:** https://adhd-and-ai.github.io/pick-your-cue/

## About the activity

- **Course:** ADHD and AI: A Different Way to Start Any Task
- **Lesson:** Module 3, Lesson 1: Build the Practice
- **Goes with:** Video 7: Choose Your Cue for the AI Assist
- **Time:** About 10 minutes

A cue is a signal that tells you it's time to act. In this activity, it's your signal to ask an AI chatbot for a different way into a task that keeps getting stuck.

## What you'll leave with

- **A cue sentence,** such as "When I open the fridge and close it again, I'll open a chatbot and paste my saved prompt."
- **A saved prompt,** ready to paste into any chatbot the next time the task stalls. The one part you update each time is clearly marked.

## How it works

You answer six short steps. A preview of your cue sentence and prompt fills in as you go.

1. **The task:** Choose a task that keeps getting stuck, or type your own.
2. **Where it stops:** Describe the moment the task stalls.
3. **Your cue:** Pick something that happens right at that moment, then check that it will work.
4. **Today's goal:** Choose one small goal. This is the part you'll change each time.
5. **Already tried:** Note what you've tried, so the chatbot suggests something new.
6. **Your result:** Copy, print, or download your cue sentence and prompt.

There are no scores, timers, or right answers. You can go back and change any step, edit the prompt directly, take a break and come back later, or start over at any time.

## Privacy

Everything stays in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

- Your answers are saved in this browser on this device, so you can pick up where you left off. They're cleared when you choose Start over, and automatically after 30 days.
- On a shared or public computer, choose Start over before you leave.
- When you type your own answers, leave out names, account numbers, and other private details. The chatbot only needs enough to understand the task.

## Accessibility

The activity is built to meet WCAG 2.2, Level AA.

- Every control works by keyboard.
- It works with screen readers.
- It works on phones and at high zoom.
- It follows your device's light or dark mode and reduced-motion settings.
- The part of the prompt that changes is marked with a text label and an underline, not by color alone.

If something doesn't work for you, please open an issue in this repository.

## For instructors and developers

The whole activity is one file, `index.html`, with no other files or setup needed. To host your own copy, upload it to any static web host, or turn on GitHub Pages for this repository (Settings > Pages > Deploy from a branch > `main`, `/ (root)`).
